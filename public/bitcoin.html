Buy bitcoin.

Pay with card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or bank. The bitcoin goes straight to your own wallet.

You will finish the purchase with a licensed provider. They verify your identity and take the payment. b.uy never holds your money or your bitcoin.

How it works. b.uy is a router, not an exchange. We send you to the provider with the best route for your amount and payment method, with your address prefilled. Minimum is usually $20 to $30. Bitcoin arrives after the provider confirms your payment, typically minutes for cards and longer for bank transfers.

Not available in your country? Coverage depends on the provider. More providers and routes are coming.
